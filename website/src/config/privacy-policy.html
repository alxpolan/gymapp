Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gymnio ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application. We want to be transparent about what stays on your device and what is sent to our service providers.

Your workouts

The workouts you log (exercises, weights, repetitions, dates) and your settings are stored on your device. Gymnio has no user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or contact information.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog (EU region) to understand how the app is used. This includes events such as opening screens, adding an exercise, or viewing the paywall, device and app information (for example system version and language), and screen recordings of the app (session replay) that help us find usability problems. The data is linked to a random identifier generated by the app, not to your name.

Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and to check whether you have an active Gymnio Pro subscription. RevenueCat receives the same random identifier and purchase information. If you came to Gymnio through an Apple Search Ads campaign, Apple's attribution information is passed to RevenueCat to measure the campaign.

AI Coach (Gymnio Pro)

The AI Coach is a Gymnio Pro feature. When you use it, the app sends a summary of your training to our server: the names of your exercises, your weights and repetitions, how many days ago you logged them, weekly totals, your language and your weight unit, together with the random identifier above. Our server forwards this summary to an AI model provider (Google Gemini, via our hosting platform Fringelo) to write your recap and tips. We do not store the summary or the generated text on our coach server. How the AI model provider handles requests is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. The coach only makes suggestions, it is not medical advice. If you do not want your training summary processed this way, do not use the AI Coach.

Data we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, or other contact information
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers

Children's Privacy

Gymnio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
